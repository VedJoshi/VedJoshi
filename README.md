## Currently

I am researching **adversarial robustness in Vision Transformers** and building a
reproducible **3D Gaussian Splatting compression** pipeline. I enjoy the point where
research constraints become engineering decisions: memory budgets, evaluation harnesses,
and systems people can actually use.

> **Field note:** a 1M-Gaussian scene can go from **236 MB to 32 MB** with a custom
> `.splat` encoder—while keeping the experiment reproducible.
